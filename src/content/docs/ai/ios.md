You are a third of the way into *Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone*, on the Hogwarts Express, and Ron is counting off his brothers. You put the book down for a week. When you come back, you cannot remember who is who.

That is what the iPhone app's AI features are for. They work from the book you are reading, they use only the pages you have read, and they answer in a sentence or two instead of a page of search results. This page follows that one reader through the book: the cast list that opens from the reader, the sky of who appears with whom, a reminder for a name, a question about the plot, the recap after a week away, and [the photo of a shelf](/ai/photo-scans).

<img src="/images/ai/ios-home.webp" alt="BookOrbit Home on iPhone with Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone at 32 percent complete and a Continue Reading button" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

:::note
Most of this needs a BookOrbit server with AI set up, which arrives with the next BookOrbit release and is covered in [Setting Up AI](/ai/connect-a-provider), and a version of the app that has these features. The app asks the server what it offers and shows only what is switched on, so a server without AI looks as it did before. Constellations works only in reflowable text books, not PDFs, comics or fixed-layout books.
:::

## Before you start

Most of this works on any iPhone, and some of it needs your server to have AI connected or your iPhone to support Apple Intelligence. [What works with your setup](/ai/overview#what-works-with-your-setup) says which, and [Which AI does each feature use?](/ai/overview#which-ai-does-each-feature-use) lists every feature. The app only offers what is available, so a feature that is missing simply is not set up for your server or phone.

Nothing goes to your server's AI until you agree to it, and the app says where each answer was written, in a line under the answer.

## Constellations

Open a book you are reading and select the **sparkles** button at the top of the reader.

<img src="/images/ai/ios-reader-controls.webp" alt="Reader for Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone with the sparkles button in the top bar and the page position at the bottom" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

**Constellations** opens as a sheet over the page. It lists everyone and everything with a name in the book, in three tabs: **People**, **Places** and **Terms**. Next to each entry is how many times it has come up so far, with the start of its description beneath the name. Above the list is a search field, a row of chips for the names on the page you are looking at, and a box for [a question about the story so far](#ask-a-question).

<img src="/images/ai/ios-constellations.webp" alt="Constellations sheet with a search field, a question box, the names on this page, People, Places and Terms tabs, and a list starting with Harry, Hagrid and Dudley" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

The **...** button holds what you can do to the whole list.

<img src="/images/ai/ios-constellations-options.webp" alt="Constellations options menu with Sort, Show, Mark names on the page, Show the whole book and Add a missing name" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

| Option | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Sort** | **Most mentioned**, **Order of appearance**, **Name** or **Recently mentioned**. |
| **Show** | **Everything so far**, **This chapter** or **This page**. |
| **Mark names on the page** | Underlines every name from the list as it appears in the reader. |
| **Show the whole book** | Turns the spoiler protection off, after asking. Useful when re-reading. Once on, the option becomes **Only pages read**. |
| **Add a missing name** | Adds a name the book spells in a way the phone did not catch, as a person, a place or a term. It shows once you have read a page that names it. |
| **Hidden names** | Appears once you have hidden one, and lists them so they can be brought back. |

Pin a name to keep it at the top, and hide a name that is not worth a place in the list.

The tabs can also link to what the book itself prints: **Cast list from the book** on People, **Maps from the book** on Places, and **Glossary from the book** on Terms, when the book has them.

<img src="/images/ai/ios-marked-names.webp" alt="A page of the book with the names of people and places underlined with dotted lines" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

### Open an entry

Select a name for its entry.

<img src="/images/ai/ios-constellations-entry.webp" alt="Entry for Hagrid showing his other names, how often he has been mentioned, a description marked as of chapter 1 part 4, and his ties" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

An entry carries:

- **Other names** the book calls them by, merged into one, so "Hagrid", "Rubeus" and "Keeper of Keys" are the same person.
- **So far in the book**, a short description written for the chapter you have reached, with the chapter it is "as of" underneath.
- **Ties** to other entries, such as who works for whom or who is family, each marked with the chapter it first appeared in.
- **Ask about this chapter**, which writes a reminder about the name from the chapter you are in. It appears only when that chapter names it, and when reading help is available.
- **Where it comes up**, **Often with** and **Your note**, below.

### Where it comes up

Scroll down and the entry shows a bar for each chapter, a marker at **You're here**, and nothing beyond it.

<img src="/images/ai/ios-constellations-timeline.webp" alt="Where it comes up chart for Hagrid with bars for chapters 1 and 2, a highlight on You're here, a flat dot for chapter 3, and tiles for first seen, busiest and latest" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

Three tiles under the chart say where the name was **First seen**, which chapter was the **Busiest** and how many mentions that was, and where it was **Latest** seen. Select **Passages** and every mention in the chapter opens as a short excerpt with the name in bold; selecting one takes the reader to that spot in the book.

<img src="/images/ai/ios-constellations-passages.webp" alt="Passages open under the chart, each a short excerpt from the chapter with Hagrid's name in bold" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

**Often with** lists the entries that turn up in the same paragraphs, and each one opens its own entry. **Your note** is a place to write who someone is to you, or a theory. In the Notebook, a name filter narrows your highlights and notes to the ones that mention a name from Constellations.

### No spoilers

Chapters you have not reached are left empty on purpose, and so is every description, tie and count. The story guide was built from the whole book, but the app only shows what comes before your page. Read on and the entries grow with you.

There are exceptions, and the sheet tells you when one applies. A finished book and a non-fiction book show everything, because there is nothing left to spoil. For re-reading, **Show the whole book** shows names and descriptions from pages you have not reached, and **Turn off** puts the protection back.

### Fix a mistake in an entry

Guides are written by a model, and a model sometimes mistakes two people for one. An entry's **...** menu lets you put it right.

<img src="/images/ai/ios-entry-menu.webp" alt="Entry menu with Pin to top, Rename, Kind, Same as, Not the same as, Edit description, Hide description, Add a tie, For everyone and Hide" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

| Action | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Pin to top** | Keeps the entry at the head of the list. |
| **Rename** | Changes the name. |
| **Kind** | Moves it between people, places and terms. |
| **Same as...** | Merges it into another entry. |
| **Not the same as...** | Separates two entries that were merged. |
| **Edit description** and **Hide description** | Corrects the written line, or removes it. |
| **Add a tie** | Joins it to another entry. |
| **For everyone** | Makes the same change for everyone who reads this book. It needs the **Build story guides** permission. |
| **Hide** | Takes it off the list. |

A fix is for you unless you make it **For everyone**. If the server refuses a fix for everyone, for example because you no longer have the permission, the app keeps it for you and says **Saved for you only**. Fixes made for everyone survive a rebuild of the guide, and ones that can no longer find what they fixed show under **Need attention**.

### If the book has no story guide

Constellations still opens on a book without a guide. The phone finds the capitalised names as you read, so you get a list of who has come up and where, but no merged names, no descriptions from the guide, and no ties.

To get the full version, someone with the **Build story guides** permission builds the guide, either on the book's page in the web app or from the **Cast** section of the book's details in the app. It arrives for everyone on the server once it is built. See [Story Guides](/ai/story-guides).

## The Sky: who appears with whom

The button at the top left of Constellations opens **the Sky**: the book's cast drawn as constellations. The most mentioned entries are the biggest stars, and the ones that share the most paragraphs are gathered together.

<img src="/images/ai/ios-sky-together.webp" alt="The Sky in Together mode, with Harry at the centre of a cluster that includes Hagrid, Hogwarts and Gringotts, another cluster around Dudley, and a third around Albus Dumbledore, and a scrubber labelled Up to this page" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

**Together** draws who appears with whom, and names the clusters, such as **Around Harry** and **Around Dudley**. **Ties** swaps those lines for the story guide's relationships. A legend names the kinds the book has, such as **Family**, **Friends and enemies** and **Other ties**, each in its own colour (love and bonds, such as a rider and a dragon, are kinds of their own), and each is a filter.

<img src="/images/ai/ios-sky-ties.webp" alt="The Sky in Ties mode, with lines between entries and a legend for Family, Friends and enemies and Other ties" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

Along the top, **All**, **People**, **Places** and **Terms** filter the stars, and the others fade. The scrubber at the bottom reads **Up to this page** and walks back through the chapters you have read: the stars you had not met yet disappear, and the others stay where they are. The play button runs the chapters one after another, so you can watch the cast grow. Once you have finished a book, the Sky can be shared as a picture.

The same protection applies here: the Sky shows nothing from beyond your page unless you have asked for the whole book.

## Ask a question

The box at the top of Constellations reads **Ask about the story so far**. Type a question, such as *Why does Harry live with the Dursleys?*, and the phone finds the paragraphs you have read that bear on it and writes an answer from only those.

<img src="/images/ai/ios-question.webp" alt="Question screen asking why Harry lives with the Dursleys, with a one-sentence answer written on the device, and below it the passages the answer was written from" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

The answer is short, says that it was written on your device, and is followed by **Passages it used**, so you can check it against the book. Select a passage to go to it. Names help: a question that mentions someone from the book finds better passages than one that does not.

Answers to typed questions get the same protection: a sentence that names someone you have not met yet is withheld, and a name the book never uses is reported as coming from outside the book. Typed questions are always answered on the phone, never by your server's AI, and they need Apple Intelligence and BookOrbit Premium. The last ten questions about a book are remembered on the phone.

## Look up a name

You do not have to open Constellations for this. Press and hold a name on the page, then select the sparkles in the menu that appears.

<img src="/images/ai/ios-select-word.webp" alt="The word Weasleys selected on the page, with the selection menu showing a sparkles button" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

A sheet slides up with the name and a short reminder.

<img src="/images/ai/ios-reminder-server.webp" alt="Sheet for Weasleys with a Book and This chapter switch, a one-paragraph reminder written by the server's AI, and an Ask More Carefully button" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

The reminder is built only from the passages you have read, shown further down the sheet so you can check it against the book. **Book** covers everything so far; **This chapter** narrows it to the chapter you are in. The line under the text says whether the phone or the server wrote it, and that it can get things wrong.

When the server wrote it and the administrator gave the stronger model a slot, **Ask More Carefully** asks again with that model. It takes longer and costs a little more, so it is there for the answer that did not satisfy you.

## Choose who writes the answers

Reminders, recaps and descriptions can be written by Apple Intelligence on your phone, or by the AI your administrator connected. You choose in **Settings > Reading Help**, separately on each device.

<img src="/images/ai/ios-settings-general.webp" alt="Settings page with Photo Scans and Reading Help rows in the General group" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

<img src="/images/ai/ios-reading-help-modes.webp" alt="Reading Help settings with three choices, On This Device Only, Server When This Device Can't and Server First, and a switch for writing Constellations descriptions automatically" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

| Choice | What happens |
|--------|--------------|
| **On This Device Only** | Apple Intelligence writes everything. No passages leave your phone. This is the default. |
| **Server When This Device Can't** | Your phone tries first. If it cannot, whether because the phone does not support Apple Intelligence, it is switched off, or it declined the passage, the server writes it. |
| **Server First** | The server writes it, and your phone steps in when the server cannot. |

The first time you pick a server choice, the app asks whether you agree.

<img src="/images/ai/ios-reading-help-consent.webp" alt="Alert titled Send Book Passages to Your Server with the buttons Send Passages and Cancel" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

Only passages from the pages you have read go to your server, which passes them to the AI provider its administrator chose. Your account, your notes, the book's title and author, and the pages you have not read stay out of it.

### What it costs you

Answers the server writes **do not need BookOrbit Premium**, and they are paid for from the server's AI budget, not by you. Premium unlocks only the answers written on the phone. If your phone cannot run Apple Intelligence, the server's AI is the way to get these features at all.

Two limits keep one reader from using up the month. Each reader has a monthly share set by the administrator, by default $1 or 300 answers, and answers already written are served from a shared cache and do not count. A reader who reaches their share gets the phone's own answer where it can write one, and a message otherwise, until the month turns.

### Descriptions on a tap

An entry with no description from a story guide can have one written for it, by your phone or by the server's AI. When the server would write it, the entry shows **Write a Description** and waits for you to tap it, so nothing is sent until you choose. To have descriptions written as you open entries, turn on **Write Descriptions Automatically** in **Settings > Reading Help**. It only matters for descriptions the server writes. Descriptions from a story guide are always there.

## Catch up with a recap

Open the reader's menu (the three dots) and select **Recap**.

<img src="/images/ai/ios-recap.webp" alt="Recap sheet titled Previously in Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone, with a The story so far paragraph and a Where you stopped paragraph" class="img-sm img-bordered" />

**The story so far** summarises the chapters behind you, taken from the story guide's chapter summaries. **Where you stopped** is a few sentences about the last pages you actually read, written fresh from them. People worth remembering are listed beneath, and **Continue Reading** closes the sheet where you left off.

Turn on **Show After a Break** in the recap's menu and it opens by itself when you return after three days or more.

## Photo scans

The same **Scan a Book** control in Search can read a cover or a whole shelf of spines and tell you which books are already in your library. It can use your server's AI for the hard cases, and you choose when under **Settings > Photo Scans**. [Photo Scans](/ai/photo-scans) walks through both modes with screenshots.

## Siri and Shortcuts

Once you have opened a book, its names are ready on the phone, and the **Who Is This in a Book** action answers a question without opening the app. Ask Siri who a name is in a book, or build it into a shortcut, and the answer comes from the description you were shown, or from who the name appears with if there is none yet. A book you have never opened says to open it first.

## If something does not work

| What you see | What to try |
|--------------|-------------|
| Entries have no description or ties | The book has no story guide yet. Ask someone who can build guides, or build one yourself if you have the permission. |
| The book page says the guide was built for a different copy | The book's file was replaced after the guide was built. Ask whoever builds guides to rebuild it. |
| "Names can only be found in books written with capital letters" | The phone finds names by their capitals, so a story guide is how books in other scripts get a cast list. |
| Selecting a name shows a Premium message | You chose **On This Device Only**, and answers written on the phone need Premium. Switch to a server choice, which is free, or add Premium. |
| A typed question cannot be answered | Questions are written on the phone, so they need Apple Intelligence turned on, and Premium. |
| **Server When This Device Can't** never uses the server | Your phone can write the answer itself, so it did. Choose **Server First** to try the server's AI. |
| "Your server doesn't offer reading help right now" | An administrator has switched **Reading help** off under **Settings > Server > AI**. |
| A message about this month's share | You reached your monthly allowance. It resets when the month turns, or an administrator can raise it. |
| Photo scans fall back to the phone | The server's **Photo scans** feature is off, its monthly budget is spent, or the Vision slot has no model that reads images. |
